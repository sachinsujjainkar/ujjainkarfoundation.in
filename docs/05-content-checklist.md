# Content Checklist

What the foundation must supply, in the order it becomes blocking. Nothing here blocks starting.

---

## Before Phase 05 (static pages) — ~3 weeks out

- [ ] **City and district of operation.** Blocking. Earlier mockups said Ujjain, MP; "उज्जैनकर" is a Marathi surname and the foundation may be in Maharashtra. Every address, dateline, and map depends on this.
- [ ] Registration type (Trust / Society), number, and date **exactly as printed** on documents
- [ ] Full postal address with pincode
- [ ] Public phone numbers and email
- [ ] Founder: name, years, photo, 150–200 words in Marathi
- [ ] Mission (ध्येय) and vision (दृष्टी), 2–3 sentences each, Marathi
- [ ] Founding year and a short history paragraph
- [ ] Final कार्यक्षेत्रे list with a one-line Marathi description each
- [ ] Three real impact statistics — three, not five, and real

## Before Phase 06 (media) — ~4 weeks out

- [ ] Logo as vector (SVG or AI/EPS). A JPEG of a logo is not a logo.
- [ ] 20–40 real photographs across कार्यक्षेत्रे. Real people, real programmes.
- [ ] Committee member portraits — consistent framing, same background if possible
- [ ] One strong horizontal hero image, minimum 2400px wide
- [ ] Marathi caption for every photo, with names where people are identifiable
- [ ] Written photo consent where children are identifiable — **required before any child's face goes on the public site**

## Before Phase 09 (newsroom) — ~5 weeks out

- [ ] Names, emails, and roles for the 3–6 staff accounts
- [ ] Who is वार्ताहर and who is संपादक — the approval chain
- [ ] 5–10 past news items in Marathi to seed the newsroom, so it does not launch empty
- [ ] Office bearers list with role titles for the `Person` records

## Before Phase 12 (launch)

- [ ] 12A and 80G certificates as PDF, plus status confirmation
- [ ] Latest audited statement or annual report
- [ ] Bank details and UPI ID for the donation page
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms — needed for the footer
- [ ] YouTube channel created, ownership confirmed
- [ ] Domain and email credentials confirmed accessible
- [ ] Named person responsible for posting weekly after launch

---

## The real risk

The build is the easy part. The common failure mode for NGO sites is that nobody posts after month two and the site quietly becomes a brochure with a stale date on it.

Decide **now**, in writing, who writes Marathi content each week and what their first ten posts will be. If that person does not exist yet, that is the highest-priority item on this list — higher than any of the above.
