## Homepage teaser (English)

Word count target: 150–200. English side of the same founder card.

A man who once relied on others' generosity to complete his own education now clears the path for thousands of students and writers to reach theirs.

Dr. Shivcharan Madhukar Ujjainkar was born on 17 November 1969 in Ghatpuri, Khamgaon, to a family of modest means — his father took up tailoring work, his mother worked as a farm labourer. Higher education seemed out of reach until Dr. K. S. Savarkar and Smt. Ratnaprabha Savarkar stepped in, enabling him to complete his B.A., M.A. (English), and B.Ed.

Since 1997 he has taught at Navin Madhyamik Vidyalaya, Parambi, while building, alongside his teaching, the Ankur Sahitya Sangh, Maharshi Valmiki Mandal, and the Ram Kadam Foundation (2009). In 2016, these efforts became the Shivcharan Ujjainkar Foundation — now a leading platform for education, literature, health, and community welfare across Maharashtra.

### बालपण आणि शिक्षण

खामगाव तालुक्यातील घाटपुरी या छोट्याशा गावात १७ नोव्हेंबर १९६९ रोजी एका सर्वसामान्य कुटुंबात शिवचरण उज्जैनकर यांचा जन्म झाला. वडील मधुकरराव उज्जैनकर शिवणकाम करून, तर आई अनुसयाबाई शेतमजुरी करून घराचा गाडा हाकत असत. तारोडा आणि रुईखेडा येथे प्राथमिक शिक्षण पूर्ण झाल्यानंतर पुढील शिक्षणासाठी आर्थिक अडचणी मोठ्या होत्या. अशा वेळी स्व. डॉ. के. एस. सावरकर आणि सौ. रत्नप्रभा कृष्ण सावरकर यांनी दिलेल्या आधारामुळे त्यांनी बी.ए., एम.ए. (इंग्रजी) आणि बी.एड. पर्यंतचे शिक्षण पूर्ण केले. हा प्रवास त्यांच्या पुढील आयुष्याची दिशा ठरवणारा ठरला — ज्याला शिक्षणासाठी संघर्ष करावा लागला, त्यानेच पुढे हजारो विद्यार्थ्यांच्या शिक्षणाचा मार्ग सुकर केला.

### शिक्षक म्हणून सेवा

१९९७ साली नवीन माध्यमिक विद्यालय, परंबी (मुक्ताईनगर) येथे त्यांनी शिक्षक म्हणून सेवेला सुरुवात केली. आजही ते याच विद्यालयात कार्यरत आहेत. विद्यार्थीप्रिय शिक्षक, प्रेरणादायी मार्गदर्शक आणि आदर्श शैक्षणिक नेतृत्व म्हणून त्यांची ओळख आहे.

### सामाजिक कार्याची सुरुवात

कुऱ्हा काकोडा येथे वास्तव्यास असताना त्यांनी पद्मश्री अण्णा हजारे यांच्या भ्रष्टाचारविरोधी आंदोलनाचे मुक्ताईनगर तालुका अध्यक्षपद तब्बल १४ वर्षे सांभाळले. पारदर्शकता, नैतिक नेतृत्व आणि सार्वजनिक हित या मूल्यांशी असलेली त्यांची बांधिलकी याच काळात अधिक दृढ झाली.

### संस्थांची उभारणी

साहित्य आणि संस्कृतीविषयीच्या ओढीतून त्यांनी अंकुर साहित्य संघ, परम पूज्य महर्षी वाल्मिकी बहुउद्देशीय मंडळ आणि कवी राम कदम फाउंडेशन (२००९) या संस्था स्थापन केल्या. या सर्व प्रयत्नांचे एकत्रीकरण होऊन २०१६ मध्ये शिवचरण उज्जैनकर फाउंडेशन अस्तित्वात आले — जे आज महाराष्ट्रभर शिक्षण, साहित्य, संस्कृती, पर्यावरण, आरोग्य आणि मानवतावादी कार्याचे आघाडीचे व्यासपीठ बनले आहे.

त्यांच्या नेतृत्वाखाली फाउंडेशनने:

- १,०००हून अधिक गुणवंत विद्यार्थ्यांचा सत्कार केला
- महाराष्ट्र व गोव्यातील १,०००हून अधिक शिक्षक, सामाजिक कार्यकर्ते, पत्रकार आणि साहित्यिकांना राज्यस्तरीय पुरस्काराने सन्मानित केले
- अनेक राज्यस्तरीय व अखिल भारतीय मराठी साहित्य संमेलने आयोजित केली
- मराठी भाषा, साहित्य, वाचनसंस्कृती आणि नवोदित लेखकांना प्रोत्साहन दिले
- शैक्षणिक मार्गदर्शन, सामाजिक जनजागृती, आरोग्य शिबिरे, पर्यावरण संवर्धन, रक्तदान आणि सामुदायिक कल्याणाचे उपक्रम राबवले

### कोविड काळातील नेतृत्व

कोविड-१९ महामारीच्या काळात डॉ. उज्जैनकर यांनी अनेक नवोपक्रम राबवले. लॉकडाऊनच्या काळात पोलीस व नगरपालिका कर्मचाऱ्यांसाठी अल्पोपाहाराची स्वतः व्यवस्था केली. फाउंडेशनने डॉक्टर, परिचारिका, पोलीस अधिकारी, सामाजिक कार्यकर्ते आणि स्वयंसेवक अशा १,४२८ कोविड योद्ध्यांचा ऑनलाइन प्रशस्तिपत्राद्वारे गौरव केला.

याच काळातील त्यांचे सर्वात उल्लेखनीय कार्य म्हणजे सलग ५ महिने २० दिवस चाललेली फेसबुक लाइव्ह व्याख्यानमाला — ज्यात भारत आणि परदेशातील १५५ मान्यवर वक्त्यांनी सहभाग घेतला. या अभूतपूर्व शैक्षणिक उपक्रमाची दखल द ग्रेट इंडियन बुक ऑफ रेकॉर्ड्सने घेतली. लॉकडाऊनच्या काळातच त्यांनी रामायण आणि महाभारतावर विस्तृत लेखनही केले — भारतीय संस्कृती व मूल्ये पुढील पिढीपर्यंत पोहोचवण्याच्या उद्देशाने.

## Full biography (English)

For the About page — donor and institutional audience.

### Early life and education

Dr. Shivcharan Ujjainkar was born on 17 November 1969 in the small village of Ghatpuri, Khamgaon taluka, Buldhana district, to a family of modest means. His father, Madhukarrao Ujjainkar, took up tailoring work to support the household; his mother, Anusayabai, worked as a farm labourer. After completing his primary schooling at Taroda and Ruikheda, further education was financially out of reach — until the late Dr. K. S. Savarkar and Smt. Ratnaprabha Krishna Savarkar offered their support, enabling him to complete his B.A., M.A. in English, and B.Ed. That early struggle for an education would come to define his life's work: he who once needed help to study would go on to open that same door for thousands of others.

### A teaching career

In 1997, Dr. Ujjainkar began teaching at Navin Madhyamik Vidyalaya in Parambi, Muktainagar — where he continues to serve today. He is widely regarded as a student-friendly teacher, an inspiring mentor, and a model of educational leadership in the region.

### The roots of social work

While living in Kurha Kakoda, Dr. Ujjainkar spent fourteen years as Muktainagar Taluka President of the anti-corruption movement inspired by Padma Shri Anna Hazare. That period sharpened his commitment to transparency, ethical leadership, and public accountability — values that would later shape the Foundation itself.

### Building an institution

His growing engagement with literature and culture led him to found the Ankur Sahitya Sangh, the Maharshi Valmiki Bahuuddeshiya Mandal, and the Ram Kadam Foundation (2009). In 2016, these efforts converged into the Shivcharan Ujjainkar Foundation, which has since grown into one of Maharashtra's leading platforms for education, literature, culture, environmental work, healthcare, and humanitarian service.

Under his leadership, the Foundation has:

- Honoured more than 1,000 meritorious students
- Recognised over 1,000 teachers, social workers, journalists, and writers across Maharashtra and Goa through state-level awards
- Organised numerous state-level and All-India Marathi Literary Conferences
- Promoted the Marathi language, literary culture, and emerging writers
- Run educational guidance programmes, health camps, environmental drives, blood donation campaigns, and community welfare initiatives

### Leadership through the pandemic

During COVID-19, Dr. Ujjainkar organised a series of community responses — personally arranging refreshments for police and municipal staff on duty through lockdown, and honouring 1,428 COVID Warriors, from doctors and nurses to police officers and volunteers, with certificates of appreciation.

His most distinctive achievement from that period was a Facebook Live lecture series running five months and twenty days, featuring 155 speakers from India and abroad — an initiative formally recognised by The Great Indian Book of Records. In the same period, he authored extensive writing on the Ramayana and Mahabharata, aimed at carrying Indian cultural values forward to the next generation.

### Recognition

Dr. Ujjainkar's work has been recognised at state, national, and international level — the Bharatiya Samaj Ratna Award from the Global Scholars Foundation, Pune, presented by Padma Shri Neelamtai Mishra; the Jalgaon Jilla Bhushan Award, presented by Padma Shri Popatrao Pawar; an honorary doctorate from South Western University, USA; and an honorary D.Litt. from Commonwealth University, South Africa-Tonga. He has also been honoured by 41 institutions across Maharashtra with awards for teaching, social service, and community contribution.

### Family

Throughout this work, Dr. Ujjainkar has had the steady support of his wife, Smt. Sangita Shivcharan Ujjainkar. Their children — Smt. Dipali Soni, Shri Sachin Ujjainkar, Smt. Shital Sachin Ujjainkar, and Shri Arvind Ujjainkar — continue the family's commitment to education and community service.

### Vision

"Education transforms individuals, literature enriches society, and selfless service builds a stronger nation."

Through the Shivcharan Ujjainkar Foundation, Dr. Ujjainkar continues to work toward a compassionate, educated, and progressive India — through education, healthcare, environmental stewardship, literature, and community service.
